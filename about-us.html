About Acebeam

Portable Lighting Built to Be Trusted

ACEBEAM was established in 2009 and its brand was founded in 2014. It focuses on high-performance portable lighting and has always been committed to promoting the development of high-power flashlight and LEP flashlight technology. For 16 years ACEBEAM has been deeply involved in the portable lighting market, providing high-quality and high-reliability lighting tools to global users. Its products cover flashlights, headlamps, tactical lights and diving lights, all backed by a 10-year warranty service.

Why Choose Acebeam
