Description

The Acebeam P20 Mini is a long-distance tactical flashlight designed around a single goal: maximum throw in a compact carry. Its SFT-60-W vertical-chip LED pushes 267806 candela downrange to an ANSI-rated 1035 meters — beam performance that used to require a full-size searchlight.

The light runs on one 21700 battery with built-in USB-C charging, so you can top up from any phone charger, wall plug or power bank. A tail switch delivers instant tactical access to strobe and turbo, while the side switch cycles through output modes with precise brightness memory.

Machined from aerospace-grade 6061-T6 aluminum with HAIII hard-anodized finish, the P20 Mini is rated IP68 — dust-tight and submersible to 2 meters. It ships with the 10-year Acebeam warranty and free shipping on orders over $49.

Owners Guide

Living With The P20 Mini

A high candela flashlight rewards a little understanding — these are the questions new owners ask first.

The P20 Mini is a deliberate compromise. Instead of chasing the biggest lumen number, it drives a tight high candela hotspot from a single 21700 cell so it can put usable light on a target a kilometre away. That makes it a USB-C flashlight you charge like a phone, and a 21700 battery flashlight you can feed with the same spare cells used across the rest of the Acebeam range.

Because throw comes from focus rather than brute force, the beam is narrow by design. For walking a trail or lighting a whole campsite, pair it with an LED headlamp or a flood-forward EDC light; for identification, search and hunting, the narrow beam is the entire point. Every P20 Mini ships with the 10-year warranty and free shipping over $49.

Charging The 21700 Cell

The included cell carries its own USB-C port, so the light charges with the same cable as your phone — no proprietary cradle. A full charge takes roughly three hours and supports power-bank output in an emergency.

Using A High Candela Flashlight

Turbo is for short bursts of identification, not for lighting a campsite. Smart thermal regulation steps output down as the head warms, which protects the emitter and keeps the light comfortable to hold.

Carry And Mounting

The pocket clip covers EDC duty, but the 1-inch body also accepts standard rail mounts and weapon-light rings — useful for hunting setups where the light has to stay fixed to the rifle.

Maintenance That Matters

Wipe the threads and re-grease the O-rings once a season, and keep the USB-C port cover seated. That is enough to preserve the IP68 seal for years of abuse.
