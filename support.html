Before You Contact Us

Having these three things ready turns most cases around in a single exchange:

  1. Your order number (it looks like ACE-2026-10482).
  2. The exact model name, plus the batch code on the box if you still have it.
  3. A short phone video of the fault — beam flicker, a dead tail switch or a charging problem is far easier to judge on screen than in words.

Do not ship anything back before you have an RMA number — parcels without one are automatically refused and returned at your expense.
