Privacy Policy

How ACEBEAM collects, uses and protects your personal data, the cookies we set, and the rights you have under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy
Last updated 23 September 2026 ACEBEAM Legal

Thank you for supporting ACEBEAM. This policy is the contract that governs how we handle your personal data when you shop at acebeam.com. We are committed to transparency, accountability and the highest standards of data protection in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to provide, protect and improve our products and services — by personalising content, offering and measuring advertisements, understanding user behaviour and providing a safer experience. You can remove or reject cookies using your browser or device settings, but doing so may affect your ability to use some parts of the store.

1.1 Definition of Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of text used to store information on web browsers. They are widely used to store and receive identifiers and other information on computers, phones and other devices.

1.2 Types of Cookies

Strictly Necessary Cookies. These are essential to provide you with fundamental services, such as visiting the site or making a purchase. If you disable them we cannot fulfil your request.

Performance Cookies. These count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of the store. All information these cookies collect is aggregated.

2. How We Use Your Data

  1. To process and deliver your order, including payment authorisation and shipping.
  2. To provide customer service, warranty repair and RMA handling.
  3. To send service emails about your order — tracking, dispatch and delivery.
  4. To send marketing email only where you have opted in, with an unsubscribe link in every message.
  5. To detect and prevent fraud, and to meet our legal and tax record-keeping duties.

3. When We Share It

We share the minimum necessary with the parties who make an order possible: payment processors, shipping carriers, and our own warehouse and support teams. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Your Rights

Under GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and request portability. Under CCPA/CPRA you may request disclosure of the categories of personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of any sale or sharing. To exercise any of these, contact our customer service team — we will verify your identity before acting.

You can opt out of marketing email at any time from the unsubscribe link in any campaign, or by asking us directly. Service emails about an order in progress will still be sent.

5. Retention & Security

We keep order records for as long as tax and consumer law require, and for as long as needed to honour the warranty on what you bought. Card details are sent straight to the payment processor and are never stored on our servers.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the store and the law change. Material changes are announced on the site; the “last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
