Choosing A Flashlight Category

If you carry a light every day, start with the EDC flashlights — light, USB-C rechargeable and easy to live with. If you need one beam for duty, hunting or search work, choose a tactical flashlight. For hands-free trail work an LED headlamp is the practical pick, and for identifying objects at extreme range shop the high power and LEP flashlights.

A tactical flashlight prioritises instant access and momentary-on with a tail switch, higher candela and a strike-ready bezel. An EDC flashlight prioritises size, weight and a pocket clip. Many Acebeam models, such as the P20 Mini, blur the line and work for both roles.

Acebeam 18650 and 21700 cells with in-light USB-C charging share the same cable across most of the range. Always match cell size and chemistry to the model listed on the product page — protected cells are recommended for the high power and LEP categories.
