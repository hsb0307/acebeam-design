Shipping Information

Yes. Orders shipped from our New Jersey warehouse (USA) or our factory warehouse in China will have tracking numbers updated within 1–2 business days, subject to time zone differences. Registered users with a valid email receive a tracking email automatically. If you checked out as a guest, contact our Customer Service team for the number. Check your spam or junk folder if the email does not arrive.

For U.S. deliveries, if items are in stock at our NJ warehouse, delivery typically takes 3–6 business days. For international orders, items ship from our China warehouse and delivery usually takes 6–10 business days via standard express shipping. Delivery to some remote countries may take longer.

If there are any irregularities in your shipping updates, please contact our Customer Service team for assistance and we will open a trace with the carrier.

Product Information

Work through these in order:
1. Remove the insulating film before first use.
2. Fully charge the battery — most cells ship low to comply with transport safety rules.
3. Check the lock/unlock function; many ACEBEAM lights have a physical or electronic lock.
4. Tighten the head, body and tail cap so there are no gaps.
5. Clean the contact points with rubbing alcohol.
6. Try a known-good original ACEBEAM battery.
If none of that helps, contact our Customer Service team.

Turbo requires sufficient battery power. If the cell is low, the driver restricts access to Turbo. Fully charge the battery and try again.

If the light turns off unexpectedly even after a full charge, the battery may be depleted or faulty. Try an original ACEBEAM battery; if the issue persists, contact support for a replacement cell.
