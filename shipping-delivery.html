Shipping & Delivery

Where Acebeam ships from, how long delivery takes, what it costs and how to track the parcel.

  1. Home
  2. Shipping & Delivery
Last updated 23 September 2026 ACEBEAM Customer Service

Orders leave either our New Jersey warehouse or our factory warehouse in Shenzhen. Standard tracked shipping is free on orders over $49, and every parcel carries a tracking number.

Delivery Times

United States: items in stock at our New Jersey warehouse typically arrive within 3–6 business days.

International (outside the U.S.): items ship from our China warehouse. Delivery usually takes 6–10 business days via standard express shipping.

Delivery to some remote countries may take longer than the ranges above. Customs clearance is outside our control and can add time.

What Shipping Costs

  • Free standard shipping on all orders over $49.
  • Express upgrade via DHL, UPS or FedEx — quoted by email within one business day.
  • Import duties and taxes, where they apply, are paid by the receiver.

Shipping Methods

All orders are shipped via standard express from either our New Jersey (USA) or China factory warehouse. You choose the speed at checkout; the warehouse is picked automatically based on what you ordered and where it is going.

Tracking Your Order

Tracking numbers are updated in your account within 1–2 business days of dispatch (this can vary with time zones). Registered users get a tracking email automatically; guest checkout customers can ask our team for the number. If the email does not arrive, check your spam or junk folder first.

You can track a parcel with any of these services:

  • 17TRACK — best for international hand-offs between carriers
  • USPS · DHL · UPS
  • FedEx · Aramex

Express Upgrade

To request expedited shipping via DHL, UPS or FedEx, email us for a quote — we respond within one business day. Express orders placed before 14:00 are dispatched the same business day.

Delayed or Missing Parcels

If the tracking stops updating or the parcel has not arrived within the window above, contact our customer service team. We will open a trace with the carrier and, where the parcel is confirmed lost, replace or refund it.

Free shipping applies to the order value after discounts and before taxes. Batteries ship separately from some regions because of carrier restrictions on lithium cells.
