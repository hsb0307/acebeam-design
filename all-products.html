Buying Guide

Reading An Acebeam Spec Sheet

Four numbers tell you almost everything about whether a flashlight suits you.

Throw Is Candela, Not Lumens

Total output measured in lumens is the headline; beam distance measured in metres follows from candela. A compact 1,000-lumen thrower can out-reach a 3,000-lumen flood light, which is why the P20 Mini identifies targets a kilometre away.

Battery Format Sets Your Routine

AA-compatible lights suit gloveboxes and emergency kits. 18650 keeps weight down. 21700 gives the best runtime-to-size ratio and most Acebeam 21700 models recharge in-light over USB-C, so one cable covers the whole family.

Flood, Spot & High Power Flashlight Beams

A narrow spot is for identification at distance; a wide flood is for walking and close work. Multi-light models such as the X35 and the Terminator M1 switch between both without swapping bodies.

Durability Ratings Are Non-Negotiable

Look for HAIII hard-anodised aluminium over 6061-T6, an IP68 waterproof seal and a 1.5-metre impact rating. Acebeam backs every one of those claims with a 10-year warranty.
