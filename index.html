Buying Guide

How To Choose An Acebeam Flashlight

Throw, output, cell type and interface: the four numbers that decide whether a light actually fits your hand and your mission.

Acebeam flashlights are engineered around one obsession: more light, further, for longer. Whether you need a compact EDC flashlight for a pocket, a tactical flashlight that survives duty use, an LED headlamp for hands-free trail work, or a high power flashlight that reaches a treeline a kilometre away, the range below starts with what the beam has to do, not with the largest number on the box.

The line-up also covers LEP flashlights built on laser-excited phosphor optics for extreme throw, dive lights sealed for submersion, and 21700 cells with USB-C charging built in. Every light ships with the 10-year Acebeam warranty and free shipping over $49.

Candela Beats Lumens For Throw

Lumens describe total output; candela describes beam intensity. A 2,000-lumen flood light and a 1,000-lumen thrower look similar on paper and behave nothing alike at 500 metres. If you must identify something far away, shop candela first.

Match The Cell To Your Routine

21700 batteries give the best runtime-to-size ratio, and most Acebeam models charge them in-light over USB-C. 18650 lights stay lighter, while AA-compatible bodies suit gloveboxes and emergency kits where a charger is never guaranteed.

Interface And Carry Decide Daily Use

A tail switch with instant strobe suits tactical and hunting roles. A side switch with mode memory is friendlier on the trail. Check the pocket clip, bezel diameter and weight before committing to carry something every day.

Insist On Real Durability Ratings

HAIII hard-anodised aluminium over 6061-T6, an IP68 seal and a 1.5-metre impact rating separate a tool from a toy. Acebeam backs every one of those claims with a 10-year warranty.
